# S5.1 — Expand Risk KPIs

## Description

Add new risk KPIs to the risk control section of the KPI engine:
blocked critical issues, aging critical issues, and issues nearing SLA.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Blocked critical count = issues priority=Critical AND status=Blocked
- [ ] Aging critical = critical issues unresolved > 30 days
- [ ] SLA-close = issues within 20% of SLA deadline
- [ ] Unassigned critical = critical issues with no assignee
- [ ] All new KPIs returned in `/api/kpi/risk` endpoint
